Wordnimals Privacy Policy Last updated: 20 October 2026 This policy explains what information the Wordnimals game ("the app") collects and how it is used. The app is published by Shine Nanda Aung. Information stored on your device Your game progress, coins, Wildlife Book, settings and daily-reward streak are stored only on your device. We do not operate accounts or servers for the app, and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or location. Advertising The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, device information and information about how you interact with ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), you will be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device's Google settings. Learn more about how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. In-app purchases Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never receive your payment card details. We only receive confirmation of which item was purchased so the app can unlock it. Children Wordnimals is a general-audience game. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Deleting your data You can delete all game data stored by the app by using Settings > Reset progress in the app, or by uninstalling the app. For data held by Google for advertising, use your device's Google settings.
